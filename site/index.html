Total Archery Challenge

The traveling mountain 3D festival — every 2027 stop, with the ones closest to the Valley called out.

Each stop runs several courses on resort or ranch terrain. You reserve a Nock Time (a course and start slot) when you register, groups are capped at six, and a stop sells out when its Nock Times are gone. There are no standings or buckles; it's a destination shoot. Tap a highlighted date or use Learn more for courses, tickets and lodging.

Ranges & shops

The ranges and pro shops that keep Arizona archers shooting — and keep our team tuned, equipped and practicing.

Each of these partners brings something to the archery community. Stop in, say you shoot with Pueblo Mechanical, and support the shops and ranges that support the sport. Hours and fees change, so check each website before you go.

Ranges

Public outdoor range · Arizona Game & Fish

Ben Avery Shooting Facility

4044 W Black Canyon Blvd, Phoenix, AZ 85086

  • NFAA-certified 5-star archery courses and a World Archery target range
  • Field course of 14 spot targets out to 80 yards; animal and hill courses with shots up to 60 yards; a 28-target hunter course
  • Daily passes online or at the Clay Target Center Pavilion, where archers check in
  • Thursday Night Indoor 3D fun shoot: $15 adults, $8 youth 8–17
  • Home to many of the state championships and series shoots on our calendar

Indoor ranges & pro shops

Indoor range & pro shop

Arizona Archery Club

1115 W Deer Valley Rd, Phoenix, AZ 85027

  • Large indoor, climate-controlled range with 28 lanes out to 40 yards, plus a kids range
  • Full pro shop with bow tuning and a demo room for trying equipment
  • Lessons, group events and the Arizona Archery Club JOAD youth program
  • Host of The Phoenix 600 (Feb 12–14, 2027) and the 2027 State JOAD Indoor Championships

Corner Archery

5008 W Northern Ave, Ste 8, Glendale, AZ 85301
